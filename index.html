All About Me

This website serves as a brief introduction to myself and the things I care about: videogames, art, and my collections.

Click Here to Learn More!

Videogames

Growing up, I was quite shy, making it difficult to make friends. Playing videogames was both a way to connect to others and a way to distract myself from loneliness. My all-time favorite franchise is the Pokemon series; I've played almost every single main series game and several spin-off games.

Art

I've been drawing off-and-on since 2020, Covid Era. I took a break when I started college in Fall of 2024, but I've been trying to get back into it over the past few months.

My Collections

I have several small collections related to my passions, some that I've been working on for years.

  • Pokemon cards
  • Ball-Jointed Dolls (BJDS)
  • My Little Pony brushable toys

Featured Art

Since 2020, my primary method of creating has been digital art via the Procreate app on my iPad. I've drawn several things over the past 6 years, but my current interest is in drawing My Little Pony characters, both canon and original. While I'm still learning and looking to improve my skills, especially in regards to anatomy and flow, I'm proud of the art I make now, given that I didn't consistently practice drawing MLP characters until a few months ago. I hope to one day be able to take the basic, cartoony style of MLP characters and use it to draw other animals and characters in a similar style.

Improving Yourself as an Artist

If you have an interest in improving your art skills, take action!

  • Youtube: Thousands of free tutorial videos for any art level.
  • Draw A Box: A free website with exercises designed to help improve your fundamental skills in art.

Advice For Fellow Collectors

One of my favorite ways to connect with fellow collectors is via social media sites, like Instagram or Tumblr. I find that these communities tend to be more welcoming and friendly than other online communities, and it gives me new ideas for displaying my collections as well as resources for finding some personal highly sought-after items.
